# Explainer Motion, Richness and Sound Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the explainer profile's reels move like the reference reel (a picture that never stands still, a new
visual every 1.5 to 3 s, scenes that build, real logos and pages, a sound bed with no silent gaps) while keeping
1080x1920 at 60 fps, free local tools, and fewer tokens per reel.

**Architecture:** Everything is built once into the composer and the scene library, so a reel costs no extra tokens:
a living layer (drifting orbs, a slow push-in on every scene), automatic transitions, six new build-up scene types, a
logo card and screenshot support, a locally generated sound bed, and a planner that turns a script with short `viz`
hints into a draft storyboard. All motion is GSAP tweens on the one paused timeline (seek-safe, deterministic); random
layouts are precomputed in Python with a fixed seed.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 (unittest), GSAP 3.14.2, HyperFrames, FFmpeg (sound), headless Chrome (screenshots),
simple-icons 13.21.0 (CC0, `npm pack`).

**Spec:** the comparison in this conversation (our reel vs the reference: 64 s of 82 s still vs 1.5 s; motion in 38% vs
92% of frames; 17 silent gaps vs none) and the user's choice: items 1 to 6, not 7; 1080p at 60 fps.

## Global Constraints

- Output stays 1080x1920 at 60 fps (`data-fps="60"`); nothing lowers resolution or frame rate.
- Free and local only: no network at compose or render; simple-icons via `npm pack simple-icons@13.21.0` once; sound
  effects and the default pad generated by FFmpeg; music only from a file the creator supplies.
- Seek-safe: no `Math.random`, `requestAnimationFrame` or timers in the page; every change is a tween on `tl`.
- GPU-light: animate transform, opacity, clip-path and SVG attributes only; no animated CSS filters, no backdrop-filter.
- The kit's checkers keep working: card-host clips with data-anchor, items with data-at, the cap-layer, one timeline.
- Ambient motion (orbs, push-in, idle floats, pulses) never counts as a "change" for pace-check.
- Every task test-first (unittest, run from `Editing-Workflow/explainer/skill/explainer/scripts`).

## Review Focus

1. A scene whose transition, idle motion and exit all touch the same element: the box gets transitions, its new
   `.live` wrapper gets the idle push, so the two never fight (Task 1/2 tests read both selectors).
2. A light background next to a dark one: the wipe reveals the new background; captions keep their colours (Task 2).
3. A storyboard with no `sound` block on a machine without FFmpeg: compose still passes with a WARN (Task 5).
4. A `viz` item whose word is not in its line: the planner spreads it evenly instead of failing (Task 6).
5. A logo slug that is not in simple-icons: compose fails naming the slug and the install line (Task 4).

### Task 3: Six build-up scenes

**Interfaces:** each module has the library contract (`TYPE, CSS, validate, height, min_width, render`), fixed
geometry (height does not depend on the text; labels are one line and `min_width` refuses one too wide):

| type | data | builds | ambient |
|---|---|---|---|
| `flow` | `nodes[{label, icon?, at}]` 2-4 | node rises in, the connector above it draws | a pulse runs down each drawn connector |
| `stack` | `items[{label, at}]` 2-5 | slabs drop onto the stack (`back.out`) | the stack bobs |
| `compare` | `left/right{title, items[{text, at}]}` ≤4 rows, `winner{side, at}?` | rows fill; the winner glows, the other dims | the winner's badge pulses |
| `timeline` | `steps[{label, sub?, at}]` 2-5 | dots light, the progress line grows step to step | the head dot pulses |
| `cards` | `cards[{title, sub?, at}]` 1-4, `focus{card, at}?` | cards deal into a fan, numbered | each card floats |
| `hub` | `center`, `items[{label, at}]` 2-6 | spokes draw, nodes pop on the ring | the ring turns slowly (labels stay upright) |

### Task 6: The draft planner and the token rules

**Files:**
- Create: `scripts/plan-storyboard.py`
- Modify: `scripts/explainer-compose.py` (WARN when a title repeats the words spoken over it),
  `agents/explainer-{writer,director,animator}.md`, `PLAYBOOK.md`, `SKILL.md`, the explainer README
- Test: `tests/test_plan.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: script.json lines (`id`, `show`, `break`, `emphasis`, `source`, new optional `viz`), transcript.json words
  with `line`.
- Produces: `plan-storyboard.py --script S --transcript T --out storyboard.json [--report PLAN.md] [--vendor DIR]`.
  A line's `viz` is `{"type": ..., <fields>}` where item lists may be plain strings; each item is anchored to the
  first word of the line matching its first word, else spread evenly. Lines without `viz`: a currency or percent
  number → `count-up`; "step N" / "number N" / "first|second|third" → `number`; a known simple-icons slug →
  `logo-card`; otherwise `title` beats of at most 2.5 s showing the emphasis word or the line's last three words. A
  `section` break moves the background along blue → black → light. Every scene's `out` is the next `in`; the last
  is `"end"`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:** a 6-line script with one `viz` flow, one "$1,000" line, one "step 2" line,
  one long plain line → the storyboard composes and pace-check passes; the flow's nodes land on their words; the
  count-up carries the line's source; the long line is split; a missing item word spreads evenly; compose WARNs on a
  title equal to its spoken words.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** → FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement**, then the docs: the Writer adds `viz` hints; the Director starts from the planner's draft
  and edits; at most one custom scene a reel; PLAYBOOK §E1.4 (a change every 1.5 to 3 s), §E4 (new scenes,
